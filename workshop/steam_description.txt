[h1]TTT Partner Mod[/h1]

[b]TTT Partner Mod[/b] (TTT PM) aims to enhance the gameplay of [url=https://www.tacticalteam.de]Tactical Training Team[/url] and its partners:

[list]
[*] [url=https://www.gruppe-w.de]Gruppe W[/url]
[*] [url=https://gruppe-adler.de/]Gruppe Adler[/url]
[*] [url=https://prae-garde.de]Praetorianische Garde[/url]
[/list]

by providing ingame access to the community logos via ACE-Tagging.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[list]
[*] [url=https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3]CBA_A3[/url]
[*] [url=https://github.com/acemod/ACE3]ACE3[/url]
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]

[list]
[*] Add a new spraypaint that allows the user to tag an object with the four community logos
[*] Add the numbers 0-9 to the black spraypaint
[/list]
